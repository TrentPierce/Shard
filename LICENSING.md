# Shard Licensing Details

## Overview
Shard is licensed under the **Functional Source License, Version 1.1, ALv2 Future License (FSL-1.1-ALv2)**. This license is designed to balance open-source transparency with commercial protection.

## What this means for you:
- **Free for Research & Development**: You can use, modify, and redistribute Shard for any purpose that isn't a "Competing Use."
- **Protected Against Hosted Clones**: You cannot use Shard to build a hosted or managed service that competes with Shard's own offerings (e.g., a managed "Shard-as-a-Service").
- **Automatic Apache 2.0 Conversion**: Every version of Shard automatically becomes open-source under the Apache License, Version 2.0 exactly two years after its release. This ensures the long-term openness of the project.

## Commercial Licensing
If you wish to use Shard in a way that would be considered a "Competing Use" under the FSL-1.1-ALv2, or if you need a license that does not have the 2-year conversion delay, please contact the Shard Project team (Barry Trenton Pierce) for a separate commercial license.

## Why FSL?
We chose the FSL because it provides:
1. **Clarity**: It's a standardized, SPDX-recognized license.
2. **Protection**: It prevents large-scale cloud providers from simply wrapping our work and selling it as a service without contributing back.
3. **Open Future**: It guarantees that the code will eventually be fully open-source (Apache 2.0), protecting the community's investment in the ecosystem.
